Webpage Layout Description for Progress Tracker
1. Overall Page Structure:

The webpage is structured in a top-down, left-to-right layout. The content is organized into three main vertical sections within a single-column structure.

2. Progress Tracker Section (Top of the Page)
2.1 Header Section (Top-Center):

Position: Top of the page, centered horizontally.
Title: "Progress Tracker" in bold.
Styling:
The word "Tracker" is underlined to emphasize the feature.
Font size is relatively large, making it the focal point of this section.
2.2 Progress Chart Area (Below Header, Centered):

Subheader Text:
"Progress chart of [sleep dropdown] over the last [30 days dropdown]"
The word "chart" and "days" are underlined, indicating interactive or editable fields.
Dropdown elements are presented as gray rectangles with a small down arrow (V).
Line Graph (Main Chart Area):
Position: Below the subheader, centered horizontally.
Graph Type: Line chart depicting sleep data over time.
Graph Elements:
X-Axis (Horizontal):
Represents time, labeled "time" at the far right.
Y-Axis (Vertical):
Represents sleep, labeled "sleep" on the left side.
Data Line:
A black wavy line showing sleep variation over a specified period.
Vertical Reference Lines:
P1S (Blue Line): Positioned at an earlier point on the timeline.
P2S (Green Line): Positioned in the middle.
P2S (Yellow Line): Positioned later, indicating another event.
Event Dots Below the X-Axis:
Dots of various colors (red, blue, green, yellow) are scattered along the horizontal axis.
These dots represent key events or data points and are aligned with corresponding graph points.
2.3 Community Position Bar (Directly Below Line Chart):

Label: "Your position vs the community" (underlined).
Horizontal Comparison Bar:
Color Gradient:
Red (Left): Represents lower percentile.
Yellow (Middle): Represents community average.
Green (Right): Represents higher percentile.
Your Position:
Marked with a blue vertical tick slightly towards the right within the green zone.
The word "you" is positioned directly above the blue marker.
Community Label: Positioned below the bar.
3. Biomarker Comparison Section (Center of the Page)
3.1 Biomarker Comparison Title (Centered):

Text: "Compare biomarkers between [dropdown date 1] and [dropdown date 2]"
Dropdown Elements:
Left: 23/04/25
Right: 30/04/25
Both dropdowns are gray rectangles with down arrows (V), indicating selection capability.
The word "between" is underlined.
Spider/Radar Chart (Below Title):
Position: Centered, directly under the comparison title.
Graph Structure:
Axes:
Top: Sleep
Right: Stress
Left: Abz
Bottom: Xyz
Graph Lines:
Red Polygon: Represents data from one date.
Blue Polygon: Represents data from another date.
The polygons form diamond shapes, indicating multi-dimensional data comparison.
Axis Lines:
Clearly labeled and evenly spaced, forming a cross-like structure.
4. Blood Test Comparison Section (Bottom of the Page)
4.1 Blood Test Comparison Title (Centered):

Text: "Compare blood tests between [dropdown date 1] and [dropdown date 2]"
Dropdown Elements:
Left: 23/04/25
Right: 30/04/25
Both dropdowns are gray rectangles with down arrows (V), indicating user interaction.
Bar Chart (Directly Below Title):
Position: Centered, below the blood test comparison title.
Graph Structure:
Two Grouped Bar Sets:
Left Group (Blue Bars): Represents data from 23/04/25.
Right Group (Yellow Bars): Represents data from 30/04/25.
Number of Bars: Two bars per group, of varying heights.
Bar Colors:
Left (Blue): Indicates older data.
Right (Yellow): Indicates more recent data.
X-Axis Labels:
Under each group, showing dates 23/04/25 and 30/04/25 respectively.
Y-Axis:
Represents measurement values, but no explicit label is shown.
5. Visual and Design Details:
Color Scheme:
Dominantly uses black, gray, blue, green, yellow, and red.
Key data points and user positions are marked with blue for easy identification.
Typography:
Bold and clear for headers.
Standard font for descriptions.
Underlining used for emphasis and indicating interactive elements.
Alignment:
Central alignment for main visual elements.
Even spacing between sections for clear separation.
Interactivity:
Dropdowns are clearly styled with gray boxes and downward arrows, indicating the ability to select alternative options.
6. Overall Usability:
The page is designed to give an at-a-glance comparison of user data with community averages.
Visual elements (charts and bars) are intuitive and color-coded, making data interpretation straightforward.
The combination of line, radar, and bar charts allows for both temporal and dimensional comparisons.
